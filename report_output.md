# Periodic Adverse Drug Experience Report (PADER)

## Executive Summary & Narrative Analysis

During the reporting period from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31, a total of 1,024 unique Individual Case Safety Reports (ICSRs) were evaluated for Bisoprolol. Among these cases, 1,023 (99.9%) were classified as serious, while 1 (0.1%) was non-serious.

The most frequently reported adverse reactions identified across the evaluated dataset include acute kidney injury (22 cases), drug ineffective (12 cases), and cerebral haemorrhage (7 cases). All metrics cited in this report originate from deterministic, verified data aggregation pipelines.
